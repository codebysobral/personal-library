# Personal Library

## Fiction Section

### The Great Gatsby — F. Scott Fitzgerald
- The story follows Jay Gatsby, a mysterious millionaire who throws 
extravagant parties in hopes of winning back his former love, Daisy.

### The Adventures of Tom Sawyer — Mark Twain
-Tom Sawyer is a mischievous and adventurous boy who gets into trouble 
and discovers exciting secrets in a small town along the Mississippi 
River.

### The Hunger Games — Suzanne Collins
- Katniss Everdeen is forced to participate in a deadly competition 
where young people fight for survival while being watched by the entire 
nation.
