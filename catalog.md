# Personal Library

## Fiction Section

### *The Great Gatsby* — F. Scott Fitzgerald

- The story follows Jay Gatsby, a mysterious millionaire who throws
  extravagant parties in hopes of winning back his former love, Daisy.

**Rating:** ★★★★☆ (4/5)

### *The Adventures of Tom Sawyer* — Mark Twain

- Tom Sawyer is a mischievous and adventurous boy who gets into trouble
  and discovers exciting secrets in a small town along the Mississippi River.

**Rating:** ★★★★☆ (4/5)

### *The Hunger Games* — Suzanne Collins

- Katniss Everdeen is forced to participate in a deadly competition
  where young people fight for survival while being watched by the entire nation.

**Rating:** ★★★★☆ (4/5)

## Technical Section

### *The Pragmatic Programmer* — Andrew Hunt & David Thomas

- A classic software engineering book that presents practical techniques
  for writing better, more maintainable code and improving problem-solving skills.

**Rating:** ★★★★☆ (4/5)

### *Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software*
— Erich Gamma, Richard Helm, Ralph Johnson & John Vlissides

- A foundational computer science book that explains common object-oriented
  design patterns used to create flexible and reusable software.

**Rating:** ★★★★☆ (4/5)

### *Introduction to Algorithms*
— Thomas H. Cormen, Charles E. Leiserson, Ronald L. Rivest & Clifford Stein

- A comprehensive textbook covering fundamental algorithms, data structures,
  sorting, graph algorithms, and algorithm analysis.

**Rating:** ★★★★☆ (4/5)
